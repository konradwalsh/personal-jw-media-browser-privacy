Privacy Policy

Personal JW Media Browser · Effective 20 August 2026

The developer does not collect, store, sell or use personal data for advertising or analytics.

About the app

Personal JW Media Browser is an independent, unofficial Android TV app for browsing and playing publicly available videos, music and audio. It is not produced by, endorsed by or affiliated with Watch Tower Bible and Tract Society of Pennsylvania or JW.org.

Data collection

The app does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. The developer does not operate a server that receives app usage or personal information.

Information stored on the device

Playback position, viewing history and app preferences may be stored locally on the user's device so that playback can resume and settings can be remembered. This information is not sent to the developer. It can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Voice search and microphone

Voice search is optional and starts only when the user selects Search by voice. The app then requests microphone permission and passes spoken audio to the speech-recognition service selected on the Android device. Depending on the device and provider settings, that service may process the audio on the device or transmit it to its provider for short-lived speech recognition. The provider's privacy terms apply.

The app receives the recognised text and uses it only to search the catalogue on the device. The app does not record or store microphone audio, retain a search history, send voice data to the developer or use it for advertising or analytics. Users can decline or revoke microphone permission and use typed search instead.

External media services

The app retrieves catalogue information and streams media directly from JW.org and related content-delivery services. As with any internet request, those external services may receive standard technical information such as the device's IP address, user agent and requested resource. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy terms.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.
